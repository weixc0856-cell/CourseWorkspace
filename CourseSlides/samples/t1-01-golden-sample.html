04 / 05 · Governing Dynamics
The Cubic Power Law: Energy Scales with Velocity Cubed
Aerodynamic Drag Force & Power Requirements:
FD = ½ · ρ · v2 · CD · A
Paero = FD · v = ½ · ρ · v3 · CD · A
• ρ: Atmospheric density (1.225 kg/m³ at 15°C, 101.3 kPa)
• CD: Drag Coefficient (dimensionless bluff shape efficiency)
• A: Frontal projected area (~2.2 m² for modern sedans, ~2.8 m² for SUVs)
Engineering Consequence

Cruising Speed vs. Power Dissipation:

  • 80 km/h → ~5.5 kW aero load
  • 100 km/h → ~10.8 kW (+96% increase)
  • 120 km/h → ~18.7 kW (+240% increase)

Every 20% increase in highway speed nearly doubles aerodynamic energy consumption, directly shrinking EV battery range.
